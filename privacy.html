Pathloose Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Liquidity Labs Studio handles information when you use Pathloose, our mobile puzzle game. It applies to the game and its connected leaderboard and challenge services.

Information we process

Game and leaderboard information. Pathloose may generate a unique player identifier and process a chosen display name (if provided), level progress, points, challenge results, rankings and related timestamps to save progress and operate online competitions. Some progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

Technical information. When the app contacts our servers, the connection requires processing technical details such as your IP address; hosting infrastructure may log timestamps, device/browser characteristics and errors for reliability and security. We do not require a real-name account to play.

Support communications. If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to include, such as a question or bug report.

How information is used

We use information to run the game, maintain individual player IDs, operate scoreboards and timed challenges, troubleshoot bugs, prevent abuse and respond to support requests. We do not sell player information to advertisers.

Advertising and purchases

Advertising. Supported versions of Pathloose may show ads provided by Google AdMob, including a banner on the home screen, interstitials between some levels, and optional rewarded videos. When ads are enabled, Google and its partners may process technical and advertising-related information, such as IP address, device and app identifiers where permitted, approximate location inferred from an IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and device characteristics. This information may be used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and personalize ads when you have given any required consent. Advertising availability varies by app version, region, and provider approval. Where required, we present consent or advertising privacy options; you may also manage device permissions in iOS Settings. We do not directly sell player information to advertisers. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

Optional in-app purchases. Supported versions may offer consumable hint packs and a weekly, automatically renewing VIP subscription. Purchases are processed by Apple's StoreKit and App Store billing system. Apple handles payment methods and billing; we do not receive payment card information. The game processes product identifiers, transaction identifiers, purchase dates, subscription entitlement and expiration information, and locally stored purchase-delivery records to grant hints, avoid duplicate deliveries, determine VIP access, and restore eligible entitlements. Prices and renewal terms are displayed before you confirm a purchase; you can manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple Settings. Learn more in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing and service providers

Limited data may be processed by hosting, infrastructure, Google AdMob, and Apple App Store/StoreKit service providers to operate the game, leaderboards, advertisements, and digital purchases. These services process data under their own privacy notices and legal obligations. We may disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service.

Retention and choices

Game progress on your device can generally be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing app data. We retain server-side player records as needed for leaderboard integrity, support, security and legal obligations. To request access, correction or deletion of information associated with your player ID, contact us using the email below. Include the player ID if available so we can locate the correct records without exposing other players' data.

Children

Pathloose is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child's personal information was provided to us, contact us and we will review the request.

International processing

Network and hosting providers may process data in locations outside your province, state or country. Applicable privacy protections and user rights continue to apply.

Updates

We may update this policy as the game changes. The date at the top will show when the policy was last revised.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, email liquiditystudio2@gmail.com.
