The essentials

Before you play

Is Pathloose free?

Pathloose is free to download. Optional in-app purchases are available, and prices are shown in the App Store and in Apple’s purchase confirmation before you pay.

How do Daily Races and Weekly Challenges work?

They’re timed and recurring challenges where you can test yourself and compare results. Online standings require an internet connection.

What if I get stuck?

You can use the game’s hint feature to help reveal your next step.
