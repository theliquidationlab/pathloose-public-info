Pathloose Support

Welcome to the official support page for Pathloose, the mobile puzzle game by Liquidity Labs Studio.

How to contact us

Email liquiditystudio2@gmail.com for questions, bug reports, or gameplay assistance. Please include your app version, phone model and a description of the problem. If helpful, attach a screenshot. Do not send passwords or sensitive account information.

Gameplay and leaderboard help

Complete puzzle levels in Play Level. Daily Race, Weekly Challenge and online Leaderboards require a working internet connection for synchronized standings. If scores seem outdated, reconnect to the internet and reopen the game.

Game progress

Some game progress and settings are stored on your device. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove locally stored progress. If you need assistance with your player ID or leaderboard records, email support with the player ID shown in the game, if available.

Purchases and advertising

The initial Pathloose 5.10.0 iOS release does not enable paid in-app purchases, subscriptions or live ads. You should not be charged for in-game items in this version.

Privacy and data requests

Read our Privacy Policy to understand what information is processed and how to request access or deletion.
